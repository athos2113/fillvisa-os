About Fillvisa

Making immigration paperwork easier, safer, and more accessible

Fillvisa exists because a basic immigration form should not require expensive software, confusing PDFs, or giving your sensitive information to yet another account.

Why it started

I am Junaid, the maker behind Fillvisa. My own visa form turned into hours of dropdowns, timeouts, broken fields, and a process that felt far harder than it needed to be. That experience became the starting point for a simpler browser-based way to prepare official immigration PDFs.

What we are building

Fillvisa is now a mission-driven form platform. The public tools stay free for immigrants and families, while professional workflows help fund a bigger roadmap that includes employment-based categories from EB-1 through EB-5.

Our principles

What Fillvisa stands for

The commitments behind every public form tool we build.

Privacy first

Immigration forms contain sensitive details. Fillvisa is designed so public-form answers stay on your device, not in our database.

Free access matters

Competitors can charge hundreds of dollars. Fillvisa keeps core form filling free so cost is not the reason someone gives up.

Useful before flashy

The best tool is the one that helps you finish. We focus on clear questions, autosave, and official-format PDF output.

Built for real journeys

Family, travel, citizenship, humanitarian, and employment pathways all deserve software that is calmer and easier to use.

Where we are going

A wider, more accessible immigration toolkit

Fillvisa began with high-friction forms people need every day. The next chapter is broader: better public tools, stronger professional workflows, and coverage that reaches family-based, travel, citizenship, humanitarian, and employment-based immigration.

Public form access

Keep essential browser-based form filling free, private, and simple for immigrants and families.

Employment pathways

Expand support for professional and employment-based workflows, including EB-1, EB-2, EB-3, EB-4, and EB-5 categories.

Professional tools

Use Fillvisa Plus to help firms collaborate, import PDFs, and build more reliable client workflows.
