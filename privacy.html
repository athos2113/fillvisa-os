Fillvisa Privacy & Data Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Privacy is not a feature we add at the end. It is one of the reasons Fillvisa exists. Public Fillvisa tools are designed to help you prepare immigration forms without uploading your private answers to us.

The short version

  • Your form answers stay in your browser on your device.
  • You do not need an account, email address, or signup to use public Fillvisa forms.
  • We do not sell your data, run session replays, or collect keystrokes.
  • This open source project does not include analytics scripts or trackers.

How public Fillvisa tools work

Fillvisa public forms run in your browser. Autosave, field validation, review, and PDF generation happen locally on your device whenever possible. Your progress may be saved in your browser's local storage so you can return later without creating an account.

Because the public tools are browser-based, you stay in control of the information you type. Clearing your browser storage or starting a new form removes locally saved progress for that browser.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit the answers you type into public Fillvisa immigration forms.

  • No names, addresses, passport numbers, A-numbers, travel history, or family details are uploaded to a Fillvisa form database.
  • No cloud sync or background upload is used for public form answers.
  • No staff member at Fillvisa can open your locally saved form answers.

Open source and no analytics

This open source version of Fillvisa does not include analytics scripts, tracking pixels, session replay tools, or hidden telemetry. You can inspect the source code yourself at athos2113/fillvisa-os.

Because there is no analytics code in this project, analytics are not used to collect:

  • Form answers
  • Uploaded immigration documents
  • Session recordings
  • Keystrokes or field-by-field personal details

Your control

  • You can clear saved progress from a form page by starting a new form.
  • You can clear browser storage for Fillvisa from your browser settings.
  • You can use private or incognito mode for a temporary session.
  • You can inspect the open source code to confirm what runs in your browser.

Fillvisa Plus and professional workflows

This policy describes the public, browser-based Fillvisa tools. Fillvisa Plus and professional services may involve accounts, collaboration, client portals, or firm-specific workflows. Those products may have additional privacy terms because their data handling is different from the free public form tools.

Important note

Fillvisa is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. We help make form preparation easier, but you are responsible for reviewing your answers and following the official instructions for your immigration situation.
