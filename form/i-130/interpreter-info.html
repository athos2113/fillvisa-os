Part 7: Interpreter's Contact Information, Certification, and Signature

Provide the following information about the interpreter if one was used.

If no interpreter assisted with this petition, you may leave this page blank.

Interpreter's Full Name

Interpreter family name is required when interpreter information is provided.
Interpreter given name is required when interpreter information is provided.

Interpreter's Mailing Address

Street number and name is required when interpreter information is provided.
Unit number is required when Apt., Ste., or Flr. is selected.
City or town is required when interpreter information is provided.
State is required for a U.S. mailing address.
Enter a valid ZIP Code.
Country is required when interpreter information is provided.

Interpreter's Contact Information

Enter a valid daytime telephone number.
Enter a valid mobile telephone number, or leave it blank.
Enter a valid email address, or leave it blank.

Interpreter's Certification

I certify, under penalty of perjury, that I am fluent in English and the following language.

Interpreter language is required when interpreter information is provided.

I have read to this petitioner in the identified language every question and instruction on this petition and the petitioner's answer to every question. The petitioner informed me that they understood every instruction, question, and answer on the petition.

Interpreter's Signature

NOTE: Use your finger or mouse to draw your signature.

Don't want to sign now? No worries - leave it blank and sign the PDF on paper or digitally later.
