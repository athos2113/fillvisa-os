Part 9: Interpreter's Contact Information, Certification, and Signature

Provide the interpreter's information if the sponsor used an interpreter in Part 8.

If no interpreter assisted with this affidavit, you may leave this page blank.

Interpreter's Full Name

Interpreter family name is required when interpreter information is required.
Interpreter given name is required when interpreter information is required.

Interpreter's Contact Information

Enter a valid daytime telephone number.
Enter a valid mobile telephone number, or leave it blank.
Enter a valid email address, or leave it blank.

Interpreter language is required when interpreter information is required.
