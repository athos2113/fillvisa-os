preparer_telephone — 4. Preparer’s Daytime Telephone Number
preparer_fax — 5. Preparer’s Fax Number (if any)
preparer_email — 6. Preparer’s Email Address (if any)

preparer_apt — Apt.
preparer_ste — Ste.
