I-751 PDF Page 3 field names
Dates: MM/DD/YYYY. Use one text field across each boxed SSN or A-Number.
State is a PDF text field containing the selected two-letter abbreviation.

BASIS-FOR-PETITION
Text fields:
Checkboxes:
joint_filing_spouse — 1.a. My spouse.
joint_filing_parent_spouse — 1.b. My parent’s spouse because I am unable to be included in a joint petition filed by my parent and my parent’s spouse.
waiver_deceased — 1.c. My spouse is deceased.
waiver_divorce — 1.d. My marriage was entered in good faith, but the marriage was terminated through divorce or annulment.
waiver_spouse_abuse — 1.e. I entered the marriage in good faith, and, during the marriage, I was battered, or was the subject of extreme cruelty, by my U.S. citizen or lawful permanent resident spouse.
waiver_parent_spouse_abuse — 1.f. My parent entered the marriage in good faith, and, during the marriage, I was battered, or was subjected to extreme cruelty, by my parent’s U.S. citizen or lawful permanent resident spouse or by my conditional resident parent.
waiver_hardship — 1.g. The termination of my status and removal from the United States would result in an extreme hardship.

SPOUSE-DETAILS
Text fields:
spouse_lastname — 2.a. Family Name (Last Name)
spouse_firstname — 2.b. Given Name (First Name)
spouse_middlename — 2.c. Middle Name (if any)
spouse_dob — 3. Date of Birth
spouse_ssn — 4. U.S. Social Security Number (if any)
spouse_a_number — 5. A-Number (if any)
spouse_physical_street — 6.a. Street Number and Name
spouse_physical_number — Unit Number
spouse_physical_city — 6.c. City or Town
spouse_physical_state — 6.d. State
spouse_physical_zip — 6.e. ZIP Code
spouse_physical_province — 6.f. Province (if applicable)
spouse_physical_postal — 6.g. Postal Code (if applicable)
spouse_physical_country — 6.h. Country
Checkboxes:
spouse_relationship_spouse — 1.a. Spouse or Former Spouse
spouse_relationship_parent_spouse — 1.b. Parent’s Spouse or Former Spouse
spouse_physical_apt — Apt.
spouse_physical_ste — Ste.
spouse_physical_flr — Flr.

CHILDREN-INFO
Text fields:
child_1_lastname — 1.a. Family Name (Last Name)
child_1_firstname — 1.b. Given Name (First Name)
child_1_middlename — 1.c. Middle Name (if any)
child_1_dob — 2. Date of Birth
child_1_a_number — 3. A-Number (if any)
Checkboxes:
child_1_living_with_you_yes — Yes
child_1_living_with_you_no — No
child_1_applying_with_you_yes — Yes
child_1_applying_with_you_no — No

Web-only: filing_type selects joint or waiver mode; has_children controls child fields. Neither needs a PDF field.
