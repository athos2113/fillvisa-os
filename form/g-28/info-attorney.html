Part 1: Information About Attorney or Accredited Representative

Enter the attorney or accredited representative's identifying information, address, and contact details.

USCIS Online Account Number

Enter a valid USCIS Online Account Number, up to 12 digits, or leave it blank.

Name of Attorney or Accredited Representative

Family name is required.
Given name is required.

Address of Attorney or Accredited Representative USPS ZIP Code Lookup

Street number and name is required.
Unit number is required when Apt., Ste., or Flr. is selected.
City or town is required.
State is required for a U.S. address.
Enter a valid ZIP Code.
Country is required.

Contact Information of Attorney or Accredited Representative

Enter a valid daytime telephone number.
Enter a valid mobile telephone number, or leave it blank.
Enter a valid email address, or leave it blank.
Enter a valid fax number, or leave it blank.
