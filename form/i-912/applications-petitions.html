Your I-912

Request for Fee Waiver

  1. I-912 Form
  2. Part 3

Part 3: Applications and Petitions for Which You Are Requesting a Fee Waiver

1. Add the form numbers of the applications and petitions for which you are requesting a fee waiver.

Applications or Petitions for You and Your Family Members

Enter A-Numbers as 7–9 digits without the A prefix, if any. List all forms being filed for each person. Leave unused family member rows blank.

You (Self)
Family member 1 (if included)
Family member 2 (if included)
Family member 3 (if included)
For each additional entry, include full name, A-Number (if any), date of birth, relationship to you, and forms being filed. These entries will go in Part 10 of the PDF.
Count every application or petition, including those listed above and in additional entries. This is the number of forms, not people.
