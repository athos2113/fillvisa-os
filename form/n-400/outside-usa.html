Part 8: Time Outside USA

List below all the trips that you have taken outside the United States during the last 5 years if you are filing based on the general provision under Part 1, Item Number 1.a. … List below all the trips that you have taken outside the United States during the last 5 years if you are filing based on the general provision under Part 1, Item Number 1.a. If you are filing based on other naturalization eligibility options, see Part 8 in the Specific Instructions by Item Number section of the Instructions for the applicable period of time for which you must enter this information. Start with your most recent trip and work backwards. Do not include day trips (where the entire trip was completed within 24 hours) in the table. If you have taken any trips outside the United States that lasted more than 6 months, see the Required Evidence – Continuous Residence section of the Instructions for evidence you should provide.
