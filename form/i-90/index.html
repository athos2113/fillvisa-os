Frequently asked questions

Is Fillvisa really free? How do you make money?

Yes — Fillvisa will always be 100% free for immigrants/travellers. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no upsells. I'm building a separate paid product for lawyers and immigration firms, so regular users never have to pay.

Is Fillvisa legal to use?

Yes. Fillvisa is a 100% legal USCIS PDF form filler that works entirely in your browser. It simply helps you complete the official I-90 form safely and correctly, then generates your filled USCIS PDF for you to review, print, and submit yourself.

Will using Fillvisa increase or decrease my chances of getting a visa?

Fillvisa helps you fill your form accurately and quickly, but the final visa decision is always made by the embassy. That said, avoiding typos, incomplete info, and formatting errors definitely helps your case.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes — your data never leaves your browser. Fillvisa is a static, frontend-only tool. There’s no database. All your information is stored locally on your device and can be cleared anytime.

Can I use Fillvisa for my family’s applications?

Absolutely. Fill out one application, then reuse the data to create forms for other family members — no need to start over every time.

Do I need to install anything to use Fillvisa?

Nope! Fillvisa works entirely in your browser — no downloads, no extensions, no Adobe Acrobat. Just open the I-90 web form, fill it out, and download your completed USCIS PDF instantly.
